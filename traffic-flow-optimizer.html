Model: fixed mode gives every road the same green and a fixed red wait (default 60 s). Smart mode uses live sensors: each road’s green is at least 8 s, extends while cars are still arriving or queued (max 45 s), ends early when the road is empty, and roads with no cars are skipped. An infrared beam across each lane about 5 cars back from the stop line asks for a green when a queue reaches it, so no lane is left waiting. Each road has 4 lanes (left-turn, two straight, right-turn). Vehicles come in six types (two-wheelers, cars, fast cars, autos, buses, trucks); faster vehicles overtake slower ones in the straight lanes. Rain slows traffic and adds a rush of extra vehicles, fog cuts visibility and speed. Demand follows a simulated Hyderabad time-of-day pattern. Traffic data is simulated/estimated, not measured. This is a classroom simulation, not a live traffic measurement.
